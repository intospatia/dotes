# Dotes Explorer on Playlab

Explore Dotes through conversation: a framework for recording lived learning experiences in your own words through **Do, Observe, Tell, Explore, Show**.

**[Open Dotes Explorer on Playlab](https://www.playlab.ai/project/cmw077m690dqlmq0wpei6oo47)**

[Playlab](https://www.playlab.ai/) is a nonprofit platform that helps educators create, share, and evaluate AI applications. Creators can shape an application's behavior, connect their own teaching and reference materials, and choose the AI model that fits their purpose.

This edition of Dotes Explorer uses **Kimi K2.5**, an **open weight model developed by Moonshot AI**. Open weight means the model's learned parameters are publicly available for download and use under its license. Playlab provides the application experience, while Kimi K2.5 provides the underlying AI model. See the [official Kimi K2.5 model card and weights](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5).

Try: **“Explain the five parts of a dote and how they fit together.”**

Created by **Michael Robbins**. Explore the Dotes framework and source materials at [dotes.xyz](https://dotes.xyz).
