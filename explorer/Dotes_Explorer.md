# Dotes Explorer

Dotes Explorer is a ChatGPT plugin for exploring Dotes, a framework for recording lived learning experiences in your own words through **Do, Observe, Tell, Explore, Show**.

Meet three fictional people, **Melu (13), Jessi (20), and Cyrus (69)**, through 63 sample dotes. Explore their stories, compare experiences across generations, learn the framework, or try writing a dote of your own. You can also see how an AI Representative could draw on permitted stories to prepare a draft, with each point traced to its source for the person to review and approve.

**[Open Dotes Explorer in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/Plugin_a0f1a9430e2481919daadc34057b79b9)**

Try: **“Introduce me to Melu and her dotes.”**

This version is a learning and demonstration tool. It does not provide a separate personal dote storage system.

Created by **Michael Robbins**. Explore the framework and source materials at [dotes.xyz](https://dotes.xyz).
